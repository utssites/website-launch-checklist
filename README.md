# Website Launch Checklist

A practical checklist to run through before and after you launch a business website. It's written for small business owners, freelancers and anyone building a site for the first time, whether you're using WordPress, Blogger, a website builder or hand-coded HTML.

Copy the list, tick things off as you go, and share it with whoever is building your site.

## 1. Plan

- [ ] Write one sentence: who is the site for, and what should visitors do (call, WhatsApp, fill a form, visit the shop)?
- [ ] Decide which pages you really need. For most small businesses, four is enough: Home, About, Services, Contact.
- [ ] Set a budget that includes the first-year renewals, not just the build.
- [ ] Look at 3 competitor sites and note what they do well and what's missing.

## 2. Domain, Hosting and Email

- [ ] Register your domain in **your own name**, not your developer's.
- [ ] Check the **renewal price** of the domain and hosting, not just the first-year offer.
- [ ] Keep the login details for your domain, hosting and website in a safe place.
- [ ] Set up a business email on your domain (for example, hello@yourbusiness.com).
- [ ] Make sure HTTPS (the padlock) is active on every page.

## 3. Content

- [ ] Write each page the way you'd explain it to a customer standing at your counter.
- [ ] Show your face on the About page, with a few honest lines on who you are and how you started.
- [ ] List your services, with starting prices if you can share them.
- [ ] Add your phone number, WhatsApp, email, address, timings and a map.
- [ ] Finish every page with a button or line that tells visitors what to do next: call, WhatsApp or ask for a quote.
- [ ] Add a short FAQ with the questions customers keep asking you on WhatsApp or at the counter.
- [ ] Add testimonials or reviews if you have them (with permission).
- [ ] Use your own photos where possible. Compress every image before uploading.

## 4. Design and Usability

- [ ] Check every page on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.
- [ ] Make sure text is easy to read (size, contrast, short paragraphs).
- [ ] Make buttons big enough to tap with a thumb.
- [ ] Limit the menu to four or five items, and keep it where visitors expect to find it.
- [ ] Run PageSpeed Insights and fix the one or two things slowing the site down most.

## 5. SEO Basics

- [ ] Don't leave pages titled just "Home" or "Services". Add your trade and city to each one, like "Services | Interior Designer in Jaipur".
- [ ] Add a one- or two-line meta description to every page, written for people rather than for search engines.
- [ ] Use a single H1 on each page, and break the rest into H2 and H3 sections.
- [ ] Short, readable URLs win: /services beats /page?id=47.
- [ ] Add alt text to every meaningful image.
- [ ] Link related pages to each other.
- [ ] Create and submit an XML sitemap.
- [ ] Type yoursite.com/robots.txt into your browser. If you see "Disallow: /" on a line of its own, Google is being told to skip your whole site, so fix it before launch.
- [ ] Add the site to Google Search Console. It's free, and it shows you how Google sees your pages.
- [ ] Claim or update your Google Business Profile and put your website link on it.

## 6. Technical Checks

- [ ] Test every link and button.
- [ ] Submit the contact form and confirm the message reaches you.
- [ ] Add a custom 404 page.
- [ ] Add a favicon (the small icon in the browser tab).
- [ ] Add analytics from day one so you can see who visits and which pages bring enquiries.
- [ ] Set up automatic backups.
- [ ] Check the site in at least two browsers.

## 7. Trust and Legal

- [ ] Add a privacy policy if you collect any visitor data (forms, analytics, email).
- [ ] Show your business name and address clearly.
- [ ] If you're registered for GST, show your GSTIN where the rules require it.
- [ ] Make sure you have the right to use every image, font and piece of text on the site.

## 8. Launch Day

- [ ] Read every page once more, out loud if you can, to catch typos.
- [ ] Drop the link into your Instagram bio, Facebook page, WhatsApp Business profile and email signature on day one.
- [ ] Tell your existing customers the site is live. A short WhatsApp message is enough.

## 9. After Launch

- [ ] Check Google Search Console after a week or two for errors.
- [ ] Update prices, photos and timings whenever they change.
- [ ] Review enquiries and visits each month, and improve the pages that perform poorly.
- [ ] Put the domain and hosting renewal dates in your calendar.
- [ ] Take a fresh backup before any big change.
